# Getting help

**Start with `STATUS.cmd`.** Double-click it. It shows what the watcher last
saw, whether the schedule is healthy, and the recent log. That answers most
questions by itself.

Then check the troubleshooting table at the bottom of
[INSTRUCTIONS.md](INSTRUCTIONS.md).

Still stuck? [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) and include the output of
`STATUS.cmd`. That plus the newest `.txt` file from your `snapshots` folder is
almost always enough to work out what's happening.

## Please don't post

- Your password. Nothing here ever needs it.
- `state/storage.json`. That file is a live sign-in to your driving school
  account — treat it like a password.
- Your ntfy topic name. Anyone who has it can read your alerts.

Snapshot files may have your child's name on them. Feel free to edit that out
before pasting; it doesn't affect the diagnosis.
