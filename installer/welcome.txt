WHAT THIS IS

Behind-the-wheel lesson openings are first come, first served, and they vanish
in minutes. This watches your driving school's scheduling page every 15 minutes
and alerts your phone the moment one appears.


WHAT IT WILL NOT DO

It never books a lesson. Grabbing a slot blind could land you a time your child
cannot make, and undoing that may cost a lesson off your package. It tells you,
opens the page, and you take it from there.

It never sees your password. You sign in yourself, in a normal browser window,
exactly as you always do.


WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

This installs the files, then opens a short setup that asks for your driving
school's web address, opens a browser so you can sign in, and offers to send
alerts to your phone. About five minutes.

It needs no administrator rights and installs only for you.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There is no account and no cost.
